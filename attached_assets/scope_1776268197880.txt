SCOPE OF SOLUTION AND REQUIREMENTS OVERVIEW
Scope of Solution:
The recommended solution is not intended to replace any current pricing methodologies.
Instead, it refactors the existing Excel-based pricing workbooks into a web-based workflow
while preserving all underlying calculation structures and logic. This document is not
intended to represent the final product design.
Requirements Overview:
This is intended as an executive summary overview. This proposal also includes an
attachment of specific User Stories and techincal requirements considerations to which we
are asking that you respond thoughtfully and thoroughly.
Objectives:
• Digitize existing Business Unit pricing and scoping workbooks into a governed,
workflow-enabled platform

Request for Quote
• Preserve pricing methodologies and calculation fidelity across all Business Units
• Support both new projects and renewal projects within a single pricing and
scoping workflow
• Enable clean multidirectional integration into Quote-to-Cash systems
Personas & Responsibilities:
• Project Delivery Lead (PDL): creates pricing models and performs initial pricing
for new and renewal projects
• Practice Area / Service Line Leadership: reviews and approves project pricing
decisions
• Pricing Operations: governs catalogs, rate tables, templates, and configuration
across Business Units
• Finance / FP&A: Sets targets, benchmarks, and guidance and validates margin
logic and consumes pricing outputs
• Risk / QRM: provides oversight and audit defensibility
• IT / Data Consumers: platform governance, retention, and downstream
integrations
End-to-End Workflow (DealPad-Aligned):
• Ability to manage and govern rate tables, hierarchies and standards within the
tool, or the ability to bring in external data tables to populate those tables (e.g,
Standard Costs rates feeding this tool via Datawarehouse; leveraging product
service mapping from CRM/Data Warehouse in place of manually adding to
pricing tool).
• Initiate pricing from an existing project context, identified as either a new project
or a renewal project.
o For renewal projects, load prior-year pricing data where available.
• Configure scope using governed scope items, assemblies, and prompts.
• Calculate pricing, costs, and margin using existing Excel workbook logic.
• Route pricing through Practice Area / Service Line Leadership approvals.
• Produce approved outputs for downstream systems and reporting.
• Examine opportunities to evolve scoping from current Excel models (i.e., client
complexity, client information, difficulty).
New Projects vs. Renewal Projects:
• Projects may be classified as either new projects or renewal projects, consistent
with the definitions and usage described in the DealPad operating model. Renewal
projects may reference prior-year pricing and historical data, while new projects
are scoped and priced without prior-year baselines.

Request for Quote
• The application must support both project types within a consistent workflow
while allowing for differences in inputs, comparisons, and approvals where
applicable.
• Scope of new project pricing vs. lead sheet (renewal) pricing have differing
considerations. Currently, there are more robust pricing considerations for new
deals compared with renewals. Due to the volume of renewals, a more
consolidated pricing workflow is needed (and sacrifices some lower level of detail
vs new pricing).
Key Assumptions and Constraints:
• Pricing methodologies are preserved, not redesigned
• Differences between new projects and renewal projects are handled through
configuration and workflow, not custom logic
• The system must reproduce calculation results consistent with existing Excel
pricing workbooks
• Acceptance is based on parity with current workbook outcomes for equivalent
inputs
