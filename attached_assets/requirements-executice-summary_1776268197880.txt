Pricing & Scoping 2.0 – Requirements Executive Summary
Application Overview & Vendor Requirements Narrative
Document Overview
This document provides a functional and behavioral requirements narrative for the Pricing
& Scoping 2.0 initiative. It is intended to be used together with the NextGenApp DealPad
operating model document, the product backlog, and the traceability matrix (User Stories).
The recommended solution is not intended to replace any current pricing methodologies.
Instead, it refactors the existing Excel-based pricing workbooks into a web-based workflow
while preserving all underlying calculation structures and logic.
This document is not intended to represent the final product design.
Objectives
• Digitize existing Business Unit pricing and scoping workbooks into a governed,
workflow-enabled platform
• Preserve pricing methodologies and calculation fidelity across all Business Units
• Support both new projects and renewal projects within a single pricing and scoping
workflow
• Enable clean downstream integration into Quote-to-Cash systems
Personas & Responsibilities
• Project Delivery Lead (PDL): creates pricing models and performs initial pricing for new
and renewal projects
• Practice Area / Service Line Leadership: reviews and approves project pricing decisions
• Pricing Operations: governs catalogs, rate tables, templates, and configuration across
Business Units
• Finance / FP&A: validates margin logic and consumes pricing outputs
• Risk / QRM: provides oversight and audit defensibility
• IT / Data Consumers: platform governance, retention, and downstream integrations
End-to-End Workflow (DealPad-Aligned)
• Initiate pricing from an existing project context, identified as either a new project or a
renewal project
• For renewal projects, load prior-year pricing data where available
• Configure scope using governed scope items, assemblies, and prompts
• Calculate pricing, costs, and margin using existing Excel workbook logic
• Route pricing through Practice Area / Service Line Leadership approvals
• Produce approved outputs for downstream systems and reporting
New Projects vs. Renewal Projects
Projects may be classified as either new projects or renewal projects, consistent with the
definitions and usage described in the DealPad operating model. Renewal projects may
reference prior-year pricing and historical data, while new projects are scoped and priced
without prior-year baselines.
The application must support both project types within a consistent workflow while
allowing for differences in inputs, comparisons, and approvals where applicable.
Key Assumptions and Constraints
• Pricing methodologies are preserved, not redesigned
• Differences between new projects and renewal projects are handled through
configuration and workflow, not custom logic
• The system must reproduce calculation results consistent with existing Excel pricing
workbooks
• Acceptance is based on parity with current workbook outcomes for equivalent inputs
